About

Two disciplines, one throughline

My grandfather spent his career as an ophthalmologic surgeon. I grew up hearing what precision under pressure actually looks like — and that's the model I'm training toward. Not just a physician, but someone who understands the tools well enough to build the ones medicine still needs.

I'm a Biomedical Engineering Honors student at UT Dallas (Collegium V Honors, National Merit Scholar) on a pre-med track. My work sits in three places at once: neuroscience research, studying how neural circuits drive behavior; applied AI, building retrieval and agent systems for clinical and research use; and direct patient care, logging the hours that will make me a better physician before I'm ever handed a white coat.

The goal isn't to pick a lane. It's to practice medicine and build the infrastructure around it.

Research

Understanding neural circuits, one photon at a time

ResearchHardware

Novel OHRBETS Assay — Dynamic Resistance Modulation

Designed and built an extension of the OHRBETS behavioral platform combining a magnetic particle brake with custom Arduino control for dynamic resistance modulation. PI-confirmed as a first-author manuscript in preparation.

ResearchTools
